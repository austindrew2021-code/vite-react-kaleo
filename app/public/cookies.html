Cookie Policy

Last updated: March 2026  |  Effective: March 2026

1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help the site function correctly, remember your preferences, and provide anonymous analytics.

2. How We Use Cookies

Xenia uses a minimal set of cookies necessary to operate the site and presale. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

4. Third-Party Cookies

When you make a card payment, you are redirected to Stripe's hosted checkout page. Stripe may set their own cookies in accordance with Stripe's Privacy Policy. We have no control over these cookies.

5. Managing Cookies

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Note that disabling cookies may affect the functionality of the site — in particular, wallet connection and presale purchase recording may not work correctly if essential cookies are disabled.

Most browsers allow you to:

  • View what cookies are stored
  • Delete cookies individually or in bulk
  • Block cookies from specific sites
  • Block all third-party cookies

6. Local Storage

In addition to cookies, we use browser local storage to store your wallet connection state and purchase history locally on your device. This data never leaves your browser and is not transmitted to our servers.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised date.
