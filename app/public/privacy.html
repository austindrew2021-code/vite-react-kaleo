Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026  |  Effective: March 2026

1. Introduction

Xenia ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard information when you visit xeniachain.com or participate in the Xenia $XEN token presale.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following information:

  • Wallet addresses — Provided when you connect a wallet or make a purchase. Used solely to deliver your $XEN token allocation.
  • Transaction data — On-chain transaction hashes and amounts, collected to verify and record your purchase.
  • Payment information — For card payments, payment processing is handled entirely by Stripe. We do not store card numbers or payment details.
  • Usage data — Anonymous analytics such as page views and session duration, collected via standard web analytics tools.
  • Email address — Only if you voluntarily provide it for updates or support.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To process and record your presale purchase
  • To deliver $XEN tokens to your wallet at the Token Generation Event
  • To respond to support inquiries
  • To send important updates about the presale or platform (if you have opted in)
  • To comply with legal obligations

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We may share data with:

  • Stripe — For payment processing (governed by Stripe's Privacy Policy)
  • Supabase — For secure database storage of purchase records
  • Legal authorities — Where required by law or to protect our legal rights

5. Blockchain Data

Please note that wallet addresses and transaction data submitted to public blockchains (Solana, Ethereum, Bitcoin, BNB Chain) are publicly visible and permanent by nature. This is outside our control.

6. Cookies

We use essential cookies required for the site to function. See our Cookie Policy for details.

7. Data Retention

We retain purchase records for as long as necessary to fulfill token delivery obligations and comply with legal requirements. You may request deletion of personal data (excluding on-chain records) by contacting us.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your personal data. Contact us at support@xeniachain.com to exercise these rights.

9. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

10. Contact

For privacy-related inquiries: support@xeniachain.com
